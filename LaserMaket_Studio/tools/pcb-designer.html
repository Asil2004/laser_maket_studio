Design a single-sided board and cut it on a CNC 3018. No PCB experience needed.

Project setup

Defaults fit a 100 × 70 mm board. Change any value and the board is planned again.

Pick your parts

Switch parts on or off. ESP, power input and converter are always needed. Every part is through-hole, so you can solder it by hand.

Your circuit

Beginner mode wired everything for you. Power goes down the tree, signals come from the ESP.

    Design rules

      CNC 3018 milling

      Three jobs run in order: cut the isolation lines, drill the holes, cut the board out. Each has its own preview.

      0.10–0.15 mm is deep enough to cut through the copper.
      1 is right for a ground-fill board.
